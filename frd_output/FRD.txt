1.5 Terminology and definitions
- Scheme: A pension scheme with a unique identifier, eligibility rules, status, and effective dates.
- Scheme identifier: The unique value used to identify a scheme.
- Material scheme change: A change to scheme configuration that is significant enough to require audit recording.
- Eligibility rules: Rules that determine whether a person or entity qualifies for a scheme.
- Audit record: A record of a material change capturing the changing user, timestamp, and old/new values.
- Duplicate scheme identity: A scheme record whose scheme identifier matches an existing scheme record.
