A. BATCH FUNCTIONAL SCOPE
This batch covers three stories within the Employer Management and Member Management epics. Supported capabilities: employer-scheme association and contribution traceability (SRC-007); controlled member enrollment with status visibility (SRC-008); and eligibility validation of enrollments with rejection handling (SRC-009). Story keys in this batch: SRC-007, SRC-008, SRC-009.

B. DETAILED FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

Capability: Employer-Scheme Association and Contribution Traceability
Jira Story Key: SRC-007
Parent Epic Key: Employer Management
Story Summary: Associate employers with schemes so contributions can be traced to the correct scheme.
Supported actor(s): pension administrator, employer.
Trigger/precondition: An employer-scheme association exists or is being created.

SRC-007-FR-01: The system shall associate an employer with one or more relevant schemes upon request by a pension administrator or employer.
Inputs and outputs: Input — employer identity and scheme identity. Output — a stored employer-scheme association record.
Missing details or ambiguities: Not specified in source — requires confirmation. Which fields uniquely identify an employer and a scheme? Is an association effective-dated? Can an employer be associated with multiple schemes simultaneously, and are some associations inactive?

SRC-007-FR-02: The system shall return all active employer-scheme associations when an employer's associations are queried.
Inputs and outputs: Input — employer identity. Output — list of active associations.
Missing details or ambiguities: Not specified in source — requires confirmation. How is "active" defined — by status flag, effective dates, or both? Are inactive or historical associations retrievable through a separate query or filter?

SRC-007-FR-03: The system shall trace a submitted contribution to the scheme associated with the contributing employer.
Inputs and outputs: Input — contribution record containing employer identity. Output — the associated scheme identity used for tracing.
Missing details or ambiguities: Not specified in source — requires confirmation. Which contribution field carries the employer identity? Is tracing performed at submission, receipt, or allocation time?

SRC-007-FR-04: The system shall flag a contribution for resolution when its employer-scheme association is missing, rather than completing it silently.
Inputs and outputs: Input — contribution record lacking a valid employer-scheme association. Output — a flagged contribution in an exception or resolution state.
Missing details or ambiguities: Not specified in source — requires confirmation. What state or status represents "flagged for resolution"? Which actors can resolve the flag, and what resolution actions are permitted?

Acceptance criteria coverage:
- Given an employer-scheme association, when a contribution is submitted by that employer, then it can be traced to the associated scheme. — Covered by SRC-007-FR-03.
- Given an employer, when its scheme associations are queried, then all active associations are returned. — Covered by SRC-007-FR-02.
- Given a contribution, when its employer-scheme association is missing, then it is flagged for resolution rather than silently completed. — Covered by SRC-007-FR-04.

Capability: Controlled Member Enrollment and Status Visibility
Jira Story Key: SRC-008
Parent Epic Key: Member Management
Story Summary: Enroll members through a controlled workflow so enrollments are validated against scheme rules before being recorded.
Supported actor(s): pension administrator, member.
Trigger/precondition: An enrollment submission is made.

SRC-008-FR-01: The system shall accept an enrollment submission and process it through a controlled workflow.
Inputs and outputs: Input — enrollment submission data. Output — an enrollment record in a workflow state.
Missing details or ambiguities: Not specified in source — requires confirmation. What workflow states exist (for example, submitted, validated, recorded, rejected)? What transitions are permitted between states?

SRC-008-FR-02: The system shall validate an enrollment against applicable scheme rules before recording it.
Inputs and outputs: Input — enrollment data and the scheme's eligibility rules. Output — validation result (pass or fail).
Missing details or ambiguities: Not specified in source — requires confirmation. Which scheme rules apply to enrollment validation? Are eligibility rules maintained separately and referenced by scheme?

SRC-008-FR-03: The system shall record an enrollment only after it has been submitted, validated against scheme rules, and approved for recording.
Inputs and outputs: Input — validated enrollment. Output — a recorded enrollment.
Missing details or ambiguities: Not specified in source — requires confirmation. Does "recorded" mean a final, immutable member record, or a provisional record subject to later change?

SRC-008-FR-04: The system shall assign a unique identity to each enrollment when it is created.
Inputs and outputs: Input — enrollment submission. Output — a unique enrollment identifier.
Missing details or ambiguities: Not specified in source — requires confirmation. Is the unique identity generated by the system, or supplied by the submitter?

SRC-008-FR-05: The system shall link each enrollment to its scheme at creation.
Inputs and outputs: Input — enrollment and scheme identity. Output — an enrollment record associated with the scheme.
Missing details or ambiguities: Not specified in source — requires confirmation. Is the scheme identity derived from the enrollment data, selected by the actor, or looked up from the employer?

SRC-008-FR-06: The system shall make the status of an in-progress enrollment visible to authorized users.
Inputs and outputs: Input — enrollment identifier and user authorization. Output — current workflow status.
Missing details or ambiguities: Not specified in source — requires confirmation. Which users are "authorized" — all pension administrators, only the submitting administrator, or scheme-specific roles? Is status visibility restricted by scheme or employer?

Acceptance criteria coverage:
- Given an enrollment submission, when it is processed, then it can be submitted, validated against scheme rules, and recorded. — Covered by SRC-008-FR-01, SRC-008-FR-02, SRC-008-FR-03.
- Given an enrollment, when it is created, then it has a unique identity and is linked to its scheme. — Covered by SRC-008-FR-04, SRC-008-FR-05.
- Given an enrollment workflow, when it is in progress, then its status is visible to authorized users. — Covered by SRC-008-FR-06.

Capability: Eligibility Validation and Rejection Handling
Jira Story Key: SRC-009
Parent Epic Key: Member Management
Story Summary: Validate enrollment against applicable scheme eligibility rules before recording, so ineligible enrollments are prevented or routed to an exception.
Supported actor(s): pension administrator, operations personnel.
Trigger/precondition: An enrollment attempt is submitted for validation.

SRC-009-FR-01: The system shall validate an enrollment attempt against applicable scheme eligibility rules before recording it.
Inputs and outputs: Input — enrollment data and scheme eligibility rules. Output — validation outcome.
Missing details or ambiguities: Not specified in source — requires confirmation. What data elements are evaluated against eligibility rules?

SRC-009-FR-02: The system shall prevent or route an enrollment to an exception when it fails eligibility rules, rather than approving it silently.
Inputs and outputs: Input — enrollment failing eligibility rules. Output — prevented enrollment or enrollment in an exception state.
Missing details or ambiguities: Not specified in source — requires confirmation. When is an enrollment prevented versus routed to an exception? What determines which path is taken?

SRC-009-FR-03: The system shall record the rejection reason when a rejected enrollment is reviewed.
Inputs and outputs: Input — rejected enrollment and reviewer. Output — recorded rejection reason.
Missing details or ambiguities: Not specified in source — requires confirmation. Is the rejection reason entered by the reviewer, generated automatically, or both? Is the reason stored on the enrollment record or in a separate audit log?

SRC-009-FR-04: The system shall proceed to enrollment record creation when an enrollment passes eligibility validation.
Inputs and outputs: Input — validated eligible enrollment. Output — enrollment record creation.
Missing details or ambiguities: Not specified in source — requires confirmation. Does passing validation automatically create the record, or does it require a separate recording action by an authorized actor?

Acceptance criteria coverage:
- Given an enrollment attempt, when it fails eligibility rules, then it is prevented or routed to an exception rather than silently approved. — Covered by SRC-009-FR-02.
- Given a rejected enrollment, when it is reviewed, then the rejection reason is recorded. — Covered by SRC-009-FR-03.
- Given an eligible enrollment, when it passes validation, then it proceeds to record creation. — Covered by SRC-009-FR-04.

C. SUPPORTED BUSINESS WORKFLOWS
No complete end-to-end workflow is fully specified in this batch. The following partial workflows are supported by the stories:

Employer-scheme association and contribution tracing (SRC-007):
1. A pension administrator or employer creates an employer-scheme association. SRC-007-FR-01.
2. The system returns all active associations for an employer on query. SRC-007-FR-02.
3. A contribution is submitted by the employer and traced to the associated scheme. SRC-007-FR-03.
4. If the employer-scheme association is missing, the contribution is flagged for resolution instead of being completed. SRC-007-FR-04.

Member enrollment workflow (SRC-008, SRC-009):
1. A pension administrator or member submits an enrollment. SRC-008-FR-01.
2. The system validates the enrollment against scheme eligibility rules. SRC-008-FR-02, SRC-009-FR-01.
3. If the enrollment passes, it proceeds to record creation. SRC-008-FR-03, SRC-009-FR-04.
4. If the enrollment fails, it is prevented or routed to an exception. SRC-009-FR-02.
5. A rejected enrollment is reviewed and the rejection reason is recorded. SRC-009-FR-03.
6. At creation, the enrollment receives a unique identity and is linked to its scheme. SRC-008-FR-04, SRC-008-FR-05.
7. While in progress, the enrollment status is visible to authorized users. SRC-008-FR-06.

D. RULES, VALIDATIONS, AND DATA
- Employer-scheme associations must exist for contributions to be traced to a scheme. SRC-007-FR-03.
- Querying an employer's scheme associations returns only active associations. SRC-007-FR-02.
- Contributions lacking an employer-scheme association must be flagged for resolution and must not be completed silently. SRC-007-FR-04.
- Enrollments must be validated against scheme eligibility rules before recording. SRC-008-FR-02, SRC-009-FR-01.
- Enrollments failing eligibility rules must be prevented or routed to an exception, not silently approved. SRC-009-FR-02.
- Rejected enrollments must have their rejection reason recorded upon review. SRC-009-FR-03.
- Eligible enrollments must proceed to record creation after passing validation. SRC-009-FR-04.
- Each enrollment must have a unique identity and be linked to its scheme at creation. SRC-008-FR-04, SRC-008-FR-05.
- Enrollment workflow status must be visible to authorized users while in progress. SRC-008-FR-06.
- No explicit field constraints, data types, or data relationships are specified in the source.
