A. BATCH FUNCTIONAL SCOPE
This batch covers the Contributions and Financial Reconciliation epic (SRC-013, SRC-014, SRC-015). Supported capabilities: recording contribution receipts and associating them with employer, member, and scheme; recording allocation outcomes and linking them to contributions and pension accounts; and reconciling submitted, received, and allocated amounts with exception flagging and variance display.

B. DETAILED FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

Capability: Contribution Receipt Recording (SRC-013)

Jira Story Key: SRC-013
Parent Epic Key: Contributions and Financial Reconciliation
Story Summary: Record contribution receipts
Supported actor(s): Finance personnel
Trigger/precondition: A contribution receipt exists and is to be recorded.
Functional requirements:
SRC-013-FR-01: The system shall record a contribution receipt when finance personnel submit receipt data.
SRC-013-FR-02: The system shall associate each recorded receipt with the relevant employer, member, and scheme.
SRC-013-FR-03: The system shall flag any difference when a receipt is matched to the corresponding submission.
Inputs and outputs:
Inputs: contribution receipt data; employer, member, and scheme identifiers; corresponding submission reference.
Outputs: recorded receipt record; receipt-to-submission match result; difference flag.
Explicit business rules and validations: Not specified in source — requires confirmation.
Exceptions/alternate paths: Not specified in source — requires confirmation.
Acceptance criteria:
Given a contribution receipt, when it is recorded, then it is associated with the relevant employer, member, and scheme.
Given a receipt, when it is matched to the corresponding submission, then any difference is flagged.
Missing details or ambiguities:

- Not specified in source — requires confirmation: the matching logic and the threshold or method used to determine a difference.

C. SUPPORTED BUSINESS WORKFLOWS
The batch supports a partial contributions lifecycle workflow, but no complete end-to-end workflow is fully specified. The supported steps, linked to story keys, are:
1. Record a contribution receipt and associate it with the relevant employer, member, and scheme (SRC-013).
2. Match the receipt to the corresponding submission and flag any difference (SRC-013).
3. Record an allocation outcome and link it to the contribution and pension account, reflecting it in the reconciliation (SRC-014).
4. Query an allocation to retrieve the associated employer, member, scheme, and pension account (SRC-014).
5. Run reconciliation of submitted, received, and allocated amounts and display exceptions and stage-by-stage variances (SRC-015).
The source does not specify the ordering constraints between receipt recording, allocation recording, and reconciliation runs, nor does it specify what happens after an exception is flagged.

D. RULES, VALIDATIONS, AND DATA
- Business data objects referenced: contribution receipt, contribution submission, allocation outcome, contribution, pension account, employer, member, scheme, reconciliation view, exception.
