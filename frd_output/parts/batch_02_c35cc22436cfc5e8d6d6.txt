A. BATCH FUNCTIONAL SCOPE
This batch covers three user stories within the Scheme Management and Employer Management epics. Supported business capabilities: auditability of material scheme configuration changes; registration and maintenance of employer records with duplicate detection; and management of authorized employer contacts with authorization enforcement. Story keys in this batch: SRC-004, SRC-005, SRC-006. Epic keys are not provided in the source records; epic summaries are "Scheme Management" (SRC-004) and "Employer Management" (SRC-005, SRC-006).

B. DETAILED FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

Capability 1: Scheme Change Auditability

Jira Story Key: SRC-004
Parent Epic Key: Not specified in source
Story Summary: Audit material scheme changes
Supported actor(s): Compliance or management reviewer (consumer of audit records); actor performing the change (not specified)
Trigger/precondition: A material scheme change is made
Functional requirements:
SRC-004-FR-01: The system shall record a material scheme change with the actor who made the change, the timestamp of the change, and the content of the change.
SRC-004-FR-02: The system shall return all material scheme changes within a specified scheme and date range when an audit query is filtered by scheme and date range.
SRC-004-FR-03: The system shall prevent alteration of a retrieved audit record without creating a new audit entry.
Inputs and outputs: Input — scheme identifier and date range for query; output — list of material scheme changes with actor, timestamp, and change content.
Explicit business rules and validations: Not specified in source — requires confirmation. Question: What defines a "material" scheme change, and is there a threshold or classification rule?
Exceptions/alternate paths: Not specified in source — requires confirmation.
Acceptance criteria:
Given a material scheme change, when it is made, then an audit record is produced with actor, timestamp, and change content.
Given an audit query, when it filters by scheme and date range, then all material changes in that range are returned.
Given an audit record, when it is retrieved, then it cannot be altered without a new audit entry.
Missing details or ambiguities: The actor performing the change is not defined; the definition of "material" is not specified; the mechanism for preventing alteration (e.g., immutability, append-only log) is not specified.

Capability 2: Employer Registration and Maintenance

Jira Story Key: SRC-005
Parent Epic Key: Not specified in source
Story Summary: Register and maintain employer records
Supported actor(s): Pension administrator or employer
Trigger/precondition: Employer registration is submitted; an existing employer record exists; an authorized actor updates an employer record
Functional requirements:
SRC-005-FR-01: The system shall record an employer registration with identifiable details when the registration is submitted.
SRC-005-FR-02: The system shall flag a registration attempt as a duplicate when the employer already exists.
SRC-005-FR-03: The system shall record an update to an employer record with the actor and timestamp when the update is performed by an authorized actor.
Inputs and outputs: Input — employer registration details; output — recorded employer record with identifiable details.
Explicit business rules and validations: Not specified in source — requires confirmation. Question: What constitutes "identifiable details" for an employer, and what matching logic determines a duplicate?
Exceptions/alternate paths: Not specified in source — requires confirmation.
Acceptance criteria:
Given an employer registration, when it is submitted, then the employer is recorded with identifiable details.
Given a registration attempt for an existing employer, when it is processed, then the duplicate is flagged.
Given an employer record, when it is updated by an authorized actor, then the change is recorded with actor and timestamp.
Missing details or ambiguities: The set of identifiable details is not specified; duplicate detection logic is not specified; the authorization mechanism for updates is not specified.

Capability 3: Authorized Employer Contacts

Jira Story Key: SRC-006
Parent Epic Key: Not specified in source
Story Summary: Manage authorized employer contacts
Supported actor(s): Employer; authorized requester
Trigger/precondition: An employer record exists; a contact is added or changed; an action is attempted on behalf of an employer; a request to view authorized contacts is made
Functional requirements:
SRC-006-FR-01: The system shall record and make traceable any addition or change to an authorized contact for an employer record.
SRC-006-FR-02: The system shall reject an action on behalf of an employer when the actor is not an authorized contact.
SRC-006-FR-03: The system shall display the authorized contact list when the requester is authorized.
Inputs and outputs: Input — contact details; output — authorized contact list.
Explicit business rules and validations: Not specified in source — requires confirmation. Question: What authorization determines whether a requester may view the contact list, and what defines an authorized contact?
Exceptions/alternate paths: Not specified in source — requires confirmation.
Acceptance criteria:
Given an employer record, when a contact is added or changed, then the change is recorded and traceable.
Given an action on behalf of an employer, when the actor is not an authorized contact, then the action is rejected.
Given a request to view authorized contacts, when the requester is authorized, then the contact list is displayed.
Missing details or ambiguities: The mechanism for recording and tracing contact changes is not specified; the authorization criteria for viewing contacts is not specified.

C. SUPPORTED BUSINESS WORKFLOWS
No complete end-to-end workflow is specified in this batch. Partial workflow fragments supported:

- Employer registration: registration submitted → employer recorded with identifiable details; duplicate attempt → flagged (SRC-005-FR-01, SRC-005-FR-02).
