A. BATCH FUNCTIONAL SCOPE
This batch covers three stories within two epics: Pension Accounts (SRC-013, SRC-014) and Contributions and Financial Reconciliation (SRC-015). Supported capabilities are account-to-member/scheme linkage, traceability of account activity to business records, and recording of contribution submissions with associations and change audit.

B. DETAILED FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

Capability: Pension Account Linkage and Traceability

Jira Story Key: SRC-013
Parent Epic Key: Pension Accounts
Story Summary: Link accounts to members and schemes
Supported actor(s): Pension administrator
Trigger/precondition: An account exists and its member and scheme links are queried.
Functional requirements:
SRC-013-FR-01: The system shall return the member and scheme links associated with a queried account.
SRC-013-FR-02: The system shall enable tracing of a contribution or investment that references an account to the relevant member and scheme.
SRC-013-FR-03: The system shall flag an account for resolution when its member or scheme links are missing or inconsistent.
Inputs and outputs: Input is an account identifier; output is the account's member and scheme linkage, traceability results, and a flag indicating missing or inconsistent links.
Explicit business rules and validations: None specified in source.
Exceptions/alternate paths: Accounts with missing or inconsistent links are flagged for resolution.
Acceptance criteria:
Given an account, when its member and scheme links are queried, then they are returned.
Given a contribution or investment, when it references an account, then it can be traced to the relevant member and scheme.
Given an account, when its links are missing or inconsistent, then it is flagged for resolution.
Missing details or ambiguities: Not specified in source — requires confirmation. Which fields constitute a missing or inconsistent link? What is the resolution workflow and who resolves flagged accounts? What defines the traceability path for contributions and investments?

Jira Story Key: SRC-014
Parent Epic Key: Pension Accounts
Story Summary: Trace account activity to business records
Supported actor(s): Compliance reviewer, management reviewer
Trigger/precondition: An account activity record exists and is queried, or an audit query is run on an account.
Functional requirements:
SRC-014-FR-01: The system shall link a queried account activity record to the relevant submission, receipt, allocation, and investment records.
SRC-014-FR-02: The system shall return all linked business records within the period specified by an audit query on an account.
SRC-014-FR-03: The system shall prevent alteration of a retrieved account activity without creating a new audit entry.
Inputs and outputs: Input is an account activity record or an account with a period; output is the set of linked business records and audit entries.
Explicit business rules and validations: None specified in source.
Exceptions/alternate paths: Any change to an account activity record must generate a new audit entry rather than modify the original.
Acceptance criteria:
Given an account activity record, when it is queried, then it links to the relevant submission, receipt, allocation, and investment records.
Given an audit query on an account, when it is run, then all linked business records within the period are returned.
Given an account activity, when it is retrieved, then it cannot be altered without a new audit entry.
Missing details or ambiguities: Not specified in source — requires confirmation. What constitutes a period for audit queries? What information must a new audit entry contain? What actions count as alteration?

Capability: Contribution Submission Recording

Jira Story Key: SRC-015
Parent Epic Key: Contributions and Financial Reconciliation
Story Summary: Record contribution submissions
Supported actor(s): Employer, pension administrator
Trigger/precondition: A contribution submission is to be recorded, modified, or queried.
Functional requirements:
SRC-015-FR-01: The system shall record a contribution submission with identifiable employer, member, and scheme associations.
SRC-015-FR-02: The system shall display the status and associations of a queried submission.
SRC-015-FR-03: The system shall record any modification to a submission with the actor and timestamp.
Inputs and outputs: Input is a contribution submission with employer, member, and scheme associations; output is the recorded submission, its status and associations, and a modification record containing actor and timestamp.
Explicit business rules and validations: None specified in source.
Exceptions/alternate paths: Modifications are recorded rather than silently overwritten.
Acceptance criteria:
Given a contribution submission, when it is recorded, then it has identifiable employer, member, and scheme associations.
Given a submission, when it is queried, then its status and associations are displayed.
Given a submission, when it is modified, then the change is recorded with actor and timestamp.
Missing details or ambiguities: Not specified in source — requires confirmation. What fields identify the employer, member, and scheme? What submission statuses exist? What is the modification workflow and who may modify submissions?

C. SUPPORTED BUSINESS WORKFLOWS
No complete end-to-end workflow is specified in this batch. The batch supports the following linked steps:
- Account linkage: a pension administrator links an account to a member and scheme (SRC-013).
- Contribution submission: an employer or pension administrator records a submission with employer, member, and scheme associations (SRC-015).
- Activity traceability: a compliance or management reviewer queries account activity and links it to submission, receipt, allocation, and investment records (SRC-014).
- Change audit: modifications to submissions and account activities are recorded with actor and timestamp and new audit entries (SRC-015, SRC-014).

D. RULES, VALIDATIONS, AND DATA
Explicit rules, validations, and data objects for this batch are limited to the following, with story keys:
- Account linkage data: account, member, and scheme associations (SRC-013).
- Account activity data: links to submission, receipt, allocation, and investment records (SRC-014).
- Submission data: employer, member, and scheme associations, status, and modification records with actor and timestamp (SRC-015).
- Auditability: account activities cannot be altered without a new audit entry (SRC-014); submission modifications are recorded with actor and timestamp (SRC-015).
No field constraints, data relationships, or status enumerations are specified in source.
