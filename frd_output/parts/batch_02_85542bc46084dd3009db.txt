A. BATCH FUNCTIONAL SCOPE
This batch covers three stories within the Employer Management and Member Management capabilities: SRC-004 (authorized employer contacts), SRC-005 (employer-to-scheme associations), and SRC-006 (member registration and enrollment). The supporting epics are Employer Management and Member Management. No epic keys are provided in the source records.

Jira Story Key: SRC-004
Parent Epic Key: Employer Management (epic key not provided in source)
Story Summary: Manage authorized employer contacts
Supported actor(s): Pension administrator
Trigger/precondition: A pension administrator initiates maintenance of an authorized contact record for an employer.

SRC-004-FR-01: The system shall store a linkage between an authorized contact and a specific employer.
Inputs: Contact identity, employer identity, linkage action.
Outputs: Stored linkage record.
Acceptance criteria: Given an authorized contact, when it is linked to a specific employer, then the linkage is stored and change history is recorded.

SRC-004-FR-02: The system shall record change history for the contact-to-employer linkage.
Acceptance criteria: Given an authorized contact, when it is linked to a specific employer, then the linkage is stored and change history is recorded.

SRC-004-FR-03: The system shall record an effective date and a reason when a contact retirement is processed.

Outputs: Processed retirement record with effective date and reason.
Acceptance criteria: Given a contact retirement, when it is processed, then an effective date and reason are recorded and the contact is no longer active.

C. SUPPORTED BUSINESS WORKFLOWS
No complete end-to-end workflow is specified in this batch. The batch supports the following partial workflows, each linked to story keys:

1. Authorized contact maintenance (SRC-004): A pension administrator links an authorized contact to an employer, the system stores the linkage and records change history; separately, a pension administrator processes a contact retirement, the system records an effective date and reason, and marks the contact as no longer active.

2. Employer-to-scheme association (SRC-005): A pension administrator establishes an employer-to-scheme association with an effective date, the system stores and makes it queryable, and historical associations remain retrievable.

3. Member enrollment (SRC-006): A pension administrator submits a member enrollment attempt; the system captures member identity and required attributes, evaluates eligibility against scheme rules, confirms eligible enrollments, and rejects or holds ineligible enrollments with the reason recorded.

D. RULES, VALIDATIONS, AND DATA
- Eligibility rule evaluation: An enrollment attempt is confirmed as eligible when it satisfies scheme eligibility rules; it is rejected or held for review when it fails scheme eligibility rules (SRC-006).
- Change history: Contact-to-employer linkages and retirements record change history (SRC-004).
- Effective dates: Contact retirements record an effective date; employer-to-scheme associations record an effective date (SRC-004, SRC-005).
- Contact status: A retired contact is marked as no longer active (SRC-004).
- Historical retrievability: Historical employer-to-scheme associations remain retrievable (SRC-005).
- Data objects implied by the source: authorized contact records, contact-to-employer linkages, contact retirement records, employer-to-scheme association records, member enrollment records, and change history records. Field-level constraints and data relationships are not specified in the source.
