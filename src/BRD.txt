# Business Requirements Document
## Pension Scheme Administration Platform
**Prepared by:** Senior Business Analysis
**Status:** Draft for Stakeholder Review
**Traceability:** Derived exclusively from the supplied Compact Business Vision

---

## 1. Document Purpose and Business Context

This Business Requirements Document (BRD) translates the supplied Compact Business Vision for the Pension Scheme Administration Platform into structured, testable business requirements. The vision describes a secure, centralized digital platform intended to replace fragmented, manual, spreadsheet-driven pension administration.

**Purpose of this document:**
*   To define the business need and intended outcomes.
*   To convert business capabilities into individually identifiable, verifiable requirements.
*   To establish the scope boundaries and confirm what remains unresolved.
*   To provide a stable reference for domain modelling, ERD creation, and design without prescribing technical architecture.

**Scope of validity:** This document represents a requirements baseline for review and approval. It distinguishes source-confirmed facts from reasonable interpretations, provisional targets, and unresolved decisions requiring stakeholder validation.

---

## 2. Executive Summary

The organization currently administers pension scheme operations using manual and spreadsheet-driven processes with limited centralized reporting and auditability. The vision is to implement a single, secure, centralized platform that supports controlled workflows across scheme administration, employer management, member management, contribution recording and reconciliation, investment records, benefits administration, and reporting.

The platform must enforce core business controls: unique identities for schemes and members, enrollment compliance with scheme eligibility rules, traceable contribution records, reconcilable submitted/received/allocated amounts, an audit trail for material changes, and explicit flagging and resolution of unmatched amounts, invalid records, and failed transactions rather than silently completing them.

The business proposes efficiency targets — a 90% reduction in manual entry, 95% data accuracy, and 99.5% platform availability — which remain provisional pending definition, baseline establishment, measurement periods, and accountable ownership.

Key unresolved decisions include the pension model type, governing regulatory jurisdiction, payment initiation versus recording only, benefit types and approval owners, integration scope, migration data and retention rules, volumes, security requirements, and role-based submission/review/approval separation.

## 3. Business Problem and Current-State Challenges

**Stated problem:** Pension administration is currently manual and spreadsheet-driven, with limited centralized reporting and auditability.

**Identified current-state challenges:**
*   **Duplicate data entry:** Manual processes create multiple records for the same underlying information.
*   **Inconsistent records:** The same data may differ across spreadsheets or manual logs.
*   **Slow reconciliation:** Matching submitted, received, and allocated contribution amounts is laborious.
*   **Limited visibility:** Operators and management cannot reliably determine processing status in real time.
*   **Weak provenance:** Difficulty proving who changed or approved a given transaction.

**Validation status:** These challenges are stated as problems to be experienced and must be validated against the actual current-state process before final requirements are baselined. This BRD treats them as the business case premise and expects confirmation of the specific pain points during stakeholder workshops.

## 5. Stakeholders and User Groups

| User Group | Business Responsibilities (as supported by the vision) |
|------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| **Pension administrators** | Primary users. Configure and maintain schemes, enroll members, record contributions, review and approve sensitive actions, and monitor reporting and exceptions. |
| **Employers** | Primary users. Register and provide authorized contacts, associate with relevant schemes, submit contributions, and view relevant status and reporting. |
| **Pension scheme members** | Primary users. Access their records and submit benefit requests. |
| **Operations personnel** | Supporting users. Manage day-to-day processing, monitor work queues, and drive exception resolution. |
| **Finance personnel** | Supporting users. Oversee contribution receipt, allocation, reconciliation, and financial reporting. |
| **Compliance personnel** | Supporting users. Monitor regulatory and internal rule compliance and support audit review. |
| **IT support** | Supporting users. Maintain platform availability, manage accounts and access, and support incident handling. |
| **Management** | Supporting users. Oversee delivery outcomes, review operational and financial reports, and make strategic decisions on scope, targets, and investments. |

**Access control requirement:** Access must be role-based, with controlled authorization for sensitive operations. Where submission, review, and approval of financial or benefit actions are required, the platform shall separate these responsibilities. ## 7. Business Capabilities and Functional Requirements

### 7.1 Capability A — Scheme Management

**Business purpose:** Establish and maintain the definitive configuration for each pension scheme so that eligibility, rules, status, and effective dates are consistent and auditable.

**Expected outcome:** A single source of truth for scheme configuration with traceability of material changes.

| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| BR-001 | The platform shall allow authorized users to create, configure, and maintain scheme details, including scheme eligibility rules, scheme rules, status, and effective dates. |
| BR-002 | The platform shall record the effective date of each scheme configuration change and maintain a chronological audit trail of material scheme changes, including who made the change and when. |
| BR-003 | The platform shall prevent or flag any scheme change that would break the agreement that each scheme has a unique identity. |

**Acceptance criteria:**
*   AC-001: A new scheme record can be created with a scheme identifier, eligibility rules, rules, status, and effective date, and cannot be duplicated for the same scheme.
*   AC-002: A material scheme change is captured with the changing user, timestamp, and old/new values, and this record is retrievable for audit.
*   AC-003: Attempting to create a duplicate scheme identity is prevented or explicitly flagged for review.

**Controls:** BR-002 (audit trail), BR-003 (unique identity), RULE-001.
**Unresolved:** Which scheme attributes are material and require explicit approval before effective change.

---

### 7.2 Capability B — Employer Management

**Business purpose:** Maintain accurate employer registration records, authorized contacts, and scheme associations so contributions can be attributed correctly.

**Expected outcome:** Reliable employer records that support contribution traceability and authorized contact routing.

| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| BR-004 | The platform shall support employer registration and record maintenance, including employer identity, status, and authorized contact details. |
| BR-005 | The platform shall allow authorized contact records for each employer to be maintained, updated, and retired with a recorded effective date and reason. |
| BR-006 | The platform shall associate each employer with the relevant scheme or schemes and record the effective date of each association. |

**Acceptance criteria:**
*   AC-004: An employer record can be created and maintained, and each employer is uniquely identifiable.
*   AC-005: An authorized contact is linked to a specific employer, with change history recorded.
*   AC-006: An employer-to-scheme association can be established and queried, and historical associations remain retrievable.

**Controls:** RULE-001, RULE-003.
**Unresolved:** Whether employer registration requires pre-approval before the employer can submit contributions.

---

### 7.3 Capability C — Member Management

**Business purpose:** Enroll eligible members, maintain member records, and prevent duplicate or invalid registrations.

**Expected outcome:** Reliable member records that are eligible by definition and free of duplicates.

| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| BR-007 | The platform shall support member registration and enrollment, capturing the member identity and required membership attributes. |
| BR-008 | The platform shall validate enrollment against the applicable scheme eligibility rules before confirming enrollment. |
| BR-009 | The platform shall support member record maintenance by authorized users, with material changes recorded for audit. |
| BR-010 | The platform shall prevent or flag the creation of duplicate or invalid member registrations. |

**Acceptance criteria:**
*   AC-007: A member enrollment attempt that satisfies scheme eligibility rules is confirmed as eligible.
*   AC-008: A member enrollment attempt that fails scheme eligibility rules is rejected or held for review, with the reason recorded.
*   AC-009: A duplicate member identity is either prevented at creation or flagged for manual review.
*   AC-010: A material member record change is recorded with the changing user, timestamp, and the prior and new values.

**Controls:** RULE-002, RULE-001, RULE-006.
**Unresolved:** What constitutes "valid" versus "invalid" registration data and the exact duplicate-detection rules.

---

### 7.4 Capability D — Pension Accounts

**Business purpose:** Administer pension-account records and their associations with members and schemes, ensuring clear traceability without defining valuation or benefit-calculation logic.

**Expected outcome:** Account records that are reliably linked to members and schemes, with traceability to the associated business records.

| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| BR-011 | The platform shall support administration of pension-account records, including each account's association with a member and a scheme. |
| BR-012 | The platform shall maintain traceability between each pension account and the relevant contribution, investment record, and benefit records. |
| BR-013 | The platform shall record relevant changes to pension-account records with an audit trail, and shall not define benefit-calculation or account-valuation logic. |

**Acceptance criteria:**
*   AC-011: Each pension account is linked to exactly one member and one scheme, and the linkage is queryable and auditable.
*   AC-012: From a pension account, the related contribution, investment, and benefit records are retrievable.
*   AC-013: Changes to a pension account are recorded with user, timestamp, and changed values, without altering any calculation logic.

**Controls:** RULE-001, RULE-003.
**Unresolved:** The definition of the account record, its lifecycle states, and whether multiple accounts per member are permitted.

---

### 7.5 Capability E — Contributions and Financial Reconciliation

**Business purpose:** Record contribution submissions and receipts, associate them with the relevant employer, member, and scheme, record allocation outcomes, reconcile differences, and track exceptions through resolution while preserving evidence of material financial actions.

**Expected outcome:** Contributions that are fully traceable and reconciled, with discrepancies and failures explicitly flagged and resolved rather than silently marked complete.

| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| BR-014 | The platform shall record contribution submissions from employers, capturing the submitted amount and submission date. |
| BR-015 | The platform shall record contribution receipts and associate each receipt with the relevant employer, member, and scheme. |
| BR-016 | The platform shall record allocation outcomes and associate each allocation with the relevant employer, member, scheme, and pension account. |
| BR-017 | The platform shall reconcile submitted, received, and allocated amounts and identify and flag any unmatched amounts, discrepancies, invalid records, and failed transactions. |
| BR-018 | The platform shall track flagged exceptions from identification through resolution and record the resolution outcome. |
| BR-019 | The platform shall preserve evidence of material financial actions, including the actor, timestamp, values, and outcome. |

**Acceptance criteria:**
*   AC-014: A submitted contribution amount can be recorded and associated with a specific employer, member, and scheme.
*   AC-015: A received contribution can be recorded and matched to the corresponding submission, with any difference flagged.
*   AC-016: An allocation outcome can be recorded, linked to the contribution and pension account, and reflected in the reconciliation.
*   AC-017: Submitted, received, and allocated amounts for a given contribution can be reconciled; any unmatched, discrepancy, invalid, or failed amounts are displayed as exceptions rather than marked complete.
*   AC-018: Each flagged exception has a trackable status from open to resolved, with the resolution record retained.
*   AC-019: A material financial action can be audited end-to-end with user, timestamp, values, and outcome.

**Controls:** RULE-003, RULE-004, RULE-006, RULE-007.
**Unresolved:** Reconciliation tolerance thresholds, what constitutes a "failed transaction," and whether failed transactions must be reversed or only flagged.

---

### 7.6 Capability F — Investment Records

**Business purpose:** Maintain approved investment and allocation records and record relevant changes while maintaining traceability.

**Expected outcome:** Reliable, auditable investment and allocation records without investment trading or portfolio execution.

| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| BR-020 | The platform shall maintain approved investment and allocation records linked to the relevant pension account, member, and scheme. |
| BR-021 | The platform shall record relevant changes to investment and allocation records and maintain traceability of who made each change and when. |
| BR-022 | The platform shall treat investment trading, portfolio execution, and financial advice as out of scope unless explicitly approved. |

**Acceptance criteria:**
*   AC-020: An approved investment record can be created, linked to a pension account, and queried.
*   AC-021: A change to an investment or allocation record is captured with user, timestamp, and changed values.
*   AC-022: No investment trading, portfolio execution, or financial-advice functionality is exposed unless separately approved.

**Controls:** RULE-005, RULE-003.
**Unresolved:** The data structure for investment records and which changes are material enough to require approval.

---

### 7.7 Capability G — Benefits Administration

**Business purpose:** Record and validate benefit requests, support controlled review and approval, and record decisions and payment outcomes.

**Expected outcome:** Benefit requests that are validated against confirmed rules, reviewed and approved through controlled workflows, with decisions and payment outcomes recorded.

| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| BR-023 | The platform shall support recording of benefit requests from members or authorized initiators, capturing request details and supporting documentation. |
| BR-024 | The platform shall validate benefit requests against confirmed scheme rules before proceeding. |
| BR-025 | The platform shall route benefit requests through a controlled review and approval workflow and record the final decision. |
| BR-026 | The platform shall track processing status for benefit requests and record payment outcomes where applicable. |

**Acceptance criteria:**
*   AC-023: A benefit request can be recorded with all required supporting details and is attributable to a member and scheme.
*   AC-024: A benefit request that does not satisfy confirmed scheme rules is flagged or rejected with the reason recorded.
*   AC-025: Benefit requests pass through review and approval stages with the decision and approver recorded.
*   AC-026: The processing status of a benefit request is visible, and any payment outcome is recorded.

**Controls:** RULE-002, RULE-007.
**Unresolved:** Which benefit types are supported, who calculates benefits, who approves them, and which benefit requests require multi-stage approval.

---

### 7.8 Capability H — Reporting and Audit

**Business purpose:** Provide operational and financial reporting, visibility into processing status and reconciliation, and traceable records of material actions for accountability and audit review.

**Expected outcome:** Reliable reports and audit records that support operational control and external audit.

| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| BR-027 | The platform shall provide operational and financial reporting to authorized users. |
| BR-028 | The platform shall provide visibility into processing status, reconciliation discrepancies, and unresolved exceptions. |
| BR-029 | The platform shall maintain traceable records of material actions and changes. |
| BR-030 | The platform shall support accountability and audit review of records, actions, and decisions. |

**Acceptance criteria:**
*   AC-027: Authorized users can generate operational and financial reports that reflect current system data.
*   AC-028: Processing status, reconciliation discrepancies, and unresolved exceptions are displayable in reports or dashboards.
*   AC-029: Material actions and changes are recorded with sufficient detail to be retrieved for audit.
*   AC-030: Audit records can be exported or presented in a format acceptable for audit review.

**Controls:** RULE-003, RULE-007.
**Unresolved:** Required report types, report frequency, retention periods for reports, and report format requirements.

## 8. Business Rules and Controls

| ID | Business Rule | Meaning | Validation / Control Behavior | Missing Information Requiring Confirmation |
|----|---------------|---------|-------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| RULE-001 | Unique identity for scheme and member | Each scheme and each member must have an agreed unique identity. | The platform shall enforce unique scheme identifiers and member identifiers, preventing duplicate creation or requiring explicit review. | The exact attribute(s) that define uniqueness and the duplicate-confirmation process. |
| RULE-002 | Enrollment and benefit compliance | Enrollment and benefit processing must comply with applicable scheme eligibility rules. | The platform shall validate enrollment and benefit requests against the confirmed eligibility rules before allowing approval or completion. | The governing country, regulator, and scheme-specific eligibility rules. |
| RULE-003 | Contribution traceability | Contributions must be traceable to the relevant employer, member, and scheme. | Every contribution submission, receipt, and allocation must be linked to a specific employer, member, and scheme, and to the pension account. | Whether multiple employers or schemes can contribute to one account. |
| RULE-004 | Reconcilability of amounts | Submitted, received, and allocated amounts must be reconcilable. | The platform shall maintain consistent amounts across submission, receipt, and allocation and provide a reconciliation view. | Reconciliation tolerance levels and mismatch handling rules. |
| RULE-005 | Investment records integrity | Investment and allocation records shall be approved and auditable; trading is out of scope. | The platform shall record approved investment allocations and changes; no trading or execution functions are included. | Which investments are supported and what constitutes an approved allocation. |
| RULE-006 | Audit trail for material changes | Material changes must have an audit trail. | The platform shall record user, timestamp, and before/after values for material changes to schemes, employers, members, accounts, contributions, investments, and benefits. | The definition of which changes are "material." |
| RULE-007 | Explicit exception handling | Unmatched amounts, invalid records, and failed transactions must be flagged and resolved rather than silently marked complete. | The platform shall display exceptions and prevent silent completion; resolution must be recorded. | What actions are permitted to resolve each exception type. |
| RULE-008 | Controlled access | Access to sensitive operations must be controlled. | The platform shall enforce role-based access control and deny unauthorized access to sensitive operations. | The final role and permission matrix. |
| RULE-009 | Separation of duties | Separation of submission, review, and approval responsibilities shall be considered for sensitive financial or benefit actions. | For sensitive actions, the submitting, reviewing, and approving actors shall be distinguishable roles, with no single user able to complete the entire chain unless approved. | Which actions require full separation and which may allow partial overlap. |

## 10. Business Processes and Exception Scenarios

The following scenarios require business definition beyond the vision.

| Scenario | Business Issue | Control Objective | Required Traceability | Unresolved Handling Decision |
|----------|----------------|-------------------|-----------------------|------------------------------|
| Duplicate or invalid member registration | A member record may already exist or contain invalid data. | Prevent or flag duplicates; avoid silent duplication. | Identity check result, reason for flag, actor who reviewed it. | Whether duplicates are blocked automatically or routed for manual review. |
| Ineligible enrollment attempt | A member may not satisfy scheme eligibility rules. | Reject or hold the enrollment and record the reason. | Eligibility rule applied, result, and actor who decided. | Whether an ineligible enrollment can be escalated for a rule exception. |
| Unmatched contribution | A submission may not match a receipt or allocation. | Flag the amount and require resolution. | Source and target amounts, mismatch amount, owner, resolution date. | What actions resolve a mismatch and whether the amount can be adjusted. |
| Submitted vs received vs allocated differences | Amounts may differ across the three stages. | Reconcile and surface discrepancies. | Stage-by-stage amounts and variance. | Tolerance thresholds for acceptable variance. |
| Failed financial transaction | A submission, receipt, or allocation may fail. | Flag the failure and prevent silent completion. | Transaction reference, failure reason, actor, timestamp. | Whether a failed transaction must be re-attempted, reversed, or only logged. |
| Missing or inconsistent records | Required data may be absent or contradictory. | Prevent processing that depends on missing data. | Missing field, expected value, actor notified. | Whether processing can proceed with an exception record. |
| Unauthorized or unapproved changes | A material change may be attempted without authorization. | Enforce role-based control and audit the attempt. | Attempted actor, change, timestamp, rejection reason. | Whether some changes can be retroactively approved. |
| Incomplete benefit request | A benefit request may lack required information. | Reject or hold the request until complete. | Missing elements, actor notified, resubmission record. | Whether requests can be reopened or must be reinitiated. |
| Unsuccessful benefit processing | A benefit request may be denied after review. | Record the denial reason and preserve the audit trail. | Decision, approver, reason, and any supporting evidence. | Whether a denied request can be appealed within the platform. |

## 12. Assumptions, Dependencies, Risks, and Constraints

### 12.1 Assumptions
*   A secure, centralized platform can replace fragmented manual and spreadsheet-based administration.
*   Stakeholders can supply scheme rules, eligibility criteria, and contribution schemas.
*   Existing records can be migrated or reconciled to the platform with acceptable data quality.

### 12.2 Dependencies
*   Scheme-specific rules and eligibility criteria.
*   Governing country, regulator, and applicable pension law.
*   Existing systems and their integration interfaces.
*   Migration data quality and completeness.
*   Stakeholder decisions on payment initiation, benefit types, and roles.

### 12.3 Risks
*   **Scope risk:** External integrations or payment initiation may be added without formal approval.
*   **Data risk:** Manual current-state records may be incomplete or inconsistent, affecting migration and accuracy targets.
*   **Regulatory risk:** The governing regulatory framework is not yet defined and may affect enrollment, contribution, and benefit rules.
*   **Control risk:** The separation-of-duties model is not yet defined, which may limit audit assurance.

### 12.4 Constraints
*   Investment trading, portfolio execution, and financial advice are out of scope unless explicitly approved.
*   Payroll processing, tax calculation, banking infrastructure, and actuarial modelling are out of scope unless approved.
*   The platform shall not invent pension regulations, calculation formulas, or scheme-specific policies.

## 15. Business Acceptance and Approval Considerations

Before the requirements are baselined for design, the following should be confirmed:

1.  **Validation of the current-state problem:** Confirm the stated manual-process challenges against actual operating evidence.
2.  **Approval of the capability set:** Confirm all eight capabilities and their boundaries.
3.  **Decision on pension model and regulatory framework:** Resolve OQ-01 and OQ-02.
4.  **Payment initiation scope:** Confirm whether the platform records or initiates payments (OQ-03).
5.  **Benefit model:** Confirm benefit types, calculation ownership, and approval owners (OQ-04).
6.  **Integration and migration strategy:** Confirm external systems, migration data, and retention rules (OQ-05, OQ-06).
7.  **Role and access model:** Confirm submission, review, approval, modification, and reconciliation roles (OQ-09).
8.  **Provisional targets:** Confirm definitions, baselines, measurement periods, methods, and owners for all three provisional targets and four additional measures.
9.  **Exception handling procedures:** Confirm how each scenario in Section 10 will be handled operationally.
10. **Non-functional requirements:** Confirm availability, recovery, security, retention, performance, and retention decisions (OQ-07, OQ-08).

Approval of this BRD does not authorize development until the unresolved questions above have been answered, and no integration, regulation, formula, workflow, or technical design shall be assumed absent explicit stakeholder confirmation.
